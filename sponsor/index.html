Sponsors & Communities

Hong Kong Open Source Conference is running on its own budget, and we cannot make it without the support from our sponsors and communities. Cordially, we would like to thank the following sponsors for their support.

Silver Sponsor

ExpressVPN

Since 2009, ExpressVPN has empowered millions of users to take control of their internet experience. The company’s award-winning consumer VPN service is backed by its open-source VPN protocol, Lightway, delivering user privacy in just a few clicks. ExpressVPN’s Keys password manager, Aircove router range, and Identity Defender tools make digital privacy and security easy and accessible for all. ExpressVPN’s products have been extensively vetted by third-party experts, including PwC, Cure53, KPMG, and others. ExpressVPN has been part of Kape Technologies since 2021. To learn more about ExpressVPN’s industry-leading privacy and security solutions, visit www.expressvpn.com

Bronze Sponsor

Y Mission

「青年薈萃」成立於2016年，2017年獲成為稅務註冊慈善機構（稅務編號：91/15081），致力為本地青年人提供自我提升、教育及社會發展機會，促進年青新一代自我承擔、服務人群、服務社會，並透過積極參與多元化社會服務，推動社會發展可持續性。

"Y Mission" was established in 2016 and obtained tax-exempt charity status in 2017 (Tax File No.: 91/15081). We are dedicated to providing local youth with opportunities for self-improvement, education, and social development, fostering a sense of responsibility, service to others, and commitment to society among the younger generation. Through active participation in diverse social services, we aim to promote sustainable social development.
